I am a Ph.D. student at the **Graduate School of Artificial Intelligence, POSTECH**,
where I am advised by **Prof. Junsuk Rho**. My research lies at the intersection of
**nanophotonics** and **artificial intelligence**: I develop deep-learning and
generative models for the **inverse design of metamaterials and metasurfaces**,
aiming to discover optical and mechanical structures that are difficult to find with
conventional approaches.

Before joining POSTECH, I received my B.S. in Biosystems Engineering (double major in
Electrical and Computer Engineering) from **Seoul National University**, graduating
*summa cum laude*.

I am broadly interested in metasurfaces, photonic and mechanical metamaterials,
electromagnetic simulation, and data-driven design.

**Research interests:** Metamaterials & metasurfaces · Nanophotonics · Deep learning & generative models for inverse design · Electromagnetic simulation (FDTD, FEM, RCWA)
